# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in OpenCarDueDiligence respectful and free
from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-focused.
- Critique ideas and changes rather than people.
- Respect privacy, consent, licensing, and the project's fail-closed safety
  boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Avoid pressuring maintainers or contributors to disclose personal case data,
  credentials, paid reports, or private conversations.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention,
discriminatory language, doxxing, publishing private information without
permission, sustained disruption, or retaliation against a reporter.

Vehicle decisions must not be based on a seller's name, ethnicity, address, or
neighborhood. Discussion should stay on verifiable vehicle, title, evidence,
and transaction facts.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project
chat, events, and when someone officially represents the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using a private contact
method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Until a dedicated
conduct address is published, do not post the incident or identifying details
in a public issue. For security or privacy vulnerabilities, use the private
process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports as confidentially and impartially as possible.
They may remove content, warn or temporarily restrict a participant, ban a
participant, or take other proportionate action. A maintainer who is involved
in a report should not be the sole decision-maker. False reports made in good
faith are not violations; knowingly fabricated reports may be.
